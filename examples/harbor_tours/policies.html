Tickets and ages

  • Adults are 16 and over. Child tickets are for ages 4 to 15.
  • Children under 4 go free and don't take a place. Book at least one adult ticket for each child under 4.
  • Children under 16 must come with an adult.
  • The Twilight Sail is for ages 12 and over, so its child tickets are for ages 12 to 15.

Booking and cancelling

  • Online booking opens 60 days ahead and closes at the end of the day before your trip.
  • For trips today, ask at the ticket hut from 9 am. We sell any places that are left.
  • You can book up to 8 places on one trip, and up to 4 trips in one reservation. For groups of 9 or more, please call us.
  • Cancel online under Manage my reservation until the end of the day before your first trip. Cancelling is free.
  • Cancelling cancels every trip in the reservation. To keep some of them, cancel and reserve those again.
  • Reservations can't be changed online. Cancel and reserve again, or call us and we'll help if we can.
  • We don't run private charters or fishing trips.

Weather

  • The skipper decides on the morning of each trip whether it is safe to sail.
  • If we cancel a trip for weather, we call or text the phone number on your reservation by 8 am, and you pay nothing.
  • The Sea Wall History Walk goes ahead in rain, but not in gales.
  • We can't tell you days ahead whether a trip will run.

Each trip's ticket prices are on our home page. Prices are in US dollars. The town sets the harbor dues. You pay at the ticket hut before you board, by card or cash. We don't take payment online, and we don't sell gift vouchers or take discount codes.

Meeting point and getting here

  • Meet at the blue ticket hut at the end of Fish Quay, 15 minutes before your trip. Boats leave on time.
  • The hut is a two-minute walk from the ferry slip.
  • Boat trips leave from the pontoon behind the hut. The Sea Wall History Walk starts at the hut.
  • We don't offer hotel or airport pickup. The airport coach stops at the ferry slip every two hours.
  • There's no parking on Fish Quay. The pay car park on Harbor Road is a five-minute walk from the hut.

What to bring

  • Warm, waterproof layers, even in summer, and flat shoes with grip.
  • We have life jackets for everyone on board, in children's sizes too.
  • The Seal Rocks Cruise goes past the harbor wall into open water, where it can be choppy. The Harbor and Lighthouse Loop stays inside the harbor.
  • Seals are wild, so we can't promise you'll see them, but we see them on most trips.

Accessibility

The Marrow Belle, which runs the Harbor and Lighthouse Loop, has step-free boarding by ramp, space for one wheelchair and an accessible toilet. The other boats need a step down or a climb from the pontoon, and the crew can help. The Sea Wall History Walk is level, with one gravel stretch. Tell us in your reservation notes what would help.

Dogs

Dogs on a lead are welcome on the Harbor and Lighthouse Loop and the Sea Wall History Walk, at no charge. We don't take dogs on the Seal Rocks Cruise, so the seals stay calm, or on the Twilight Sail. Assistance dogs are welcome on every trip.
